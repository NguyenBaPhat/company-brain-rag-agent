# Customer Survey — Q4 2025 Summary

Method: online survey of 1,240 US skincare buyers aged 22–44 (existing Lumera customers n=420, lookalike non-customers n=820), fielded 3–17 November 2025.

## Purchase Drivers (top 4, multiple answers allowed)
1. Ingredient transparency — 41%
2. Suitability for sensitive skin — 37%
3. Price and value — 29%
4. Peer reviews and ratings — 26%

## Pain Points
- 58% experienced irritation (stinging, redness) from a skincare product in the last 12 months.
- 46% have abandoned a skincare routine because it had too many steps.
- 63% say they distrust before-and-after photos they see online.
- 71% say they trust peer reviews more than brand advertising.

## Customer Language
Customers describe their needs with phrases such as "my skin barrier is wrecked", "it stings", "it pills under sunscreen", "I just want three steps", and "I read the ingredient list first". Using this language in hooks reads as native rather than advertorial.

## Channels
Instagram is used weekly by 52% of respondents, TikTok by 38%, and YouTube by 21%. Among Lumera customers, 33% first discovered the brand through a creator video.

## Limitations
The sample is US-only. No data was collected on customers outside the US, on men over 45, or on purchase behaviour in retail stores.
