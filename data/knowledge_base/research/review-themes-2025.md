# Customer Review and Support Feedback Themes — Jan to Sep 2025

Source: 2,100 on-site product reviews and 640 support tickets, January–September 2025. Average rating across products: 4.6 out of 5.

## Positive Themes (share of reviews mentioning)
- Lightweight, non-sticky texture — 34%
- "No stinging" or "didn't irritate my skin" — 22%
- Visible brightness within 2–3 weeks (Glow Serum) — 19%
- Layers well under sunscreen and makeup, no pilling — 11%
- Fragrance-free — 9%

## Negative Themes
- Slow delivery — 7%
- Pump dispenser clogging or under-dispensing (Glow Serum) — 6%
- Barrier Cream jar leaking or arriving with a loose lid — 4%
- "No visible effect on my dark spots" — 5% (an expectations gap: we do not claim to treat dark spots)

## Support Ticket Themes
- Order status and delivery questions — 38% of tickets
- Subscription changes and cancellation — 21%
- Product-use questions (order of application, SPF) — 17%
- Reaction or irritation reports — 3% of tickets, all escalated per the adverse reaction SOP

## Insight For Creative
Customers repeat the same three proof points in their own words: texture, no stinging, and a visible glow after a few weeks. Timeframe language ("after 3 weeks") appears in 27% of positive reviews.

## Caveat
Review text is self-selected and may not represent all customers. Direct quotes may only be used in ads with the reviewer's permission.
