# Core Customer Personas

Personas are built from the Q4 2025 survey and customer interviews (n=18).

## Sensitive-Skin Sara
- Age 28–38, urban, works in a professional role.
- Skin reacts easily; reads the INCI ingredient list before buying.
- Pain points: fear of stinging and redness, burned by "strong" actives before.
- Wants: gentle formulas, transparent ingredients, reassurance.
- Best-fit products: Barrier Cream, Glow Serum.
- Trigger message: "Glow, without the stinging."

## Routine-Minimalist Minh
- Age 24–34, time-poor, values efficiency and fair price.
- Has abandoned long routines. Wants three steps maximum.
- Best-fit offer: Starter Trio ($79).
- Trigger message: "Three steps. Zero drama."

## Glow-Seeking Gia
- Age 22–30, TikTok-first, responds to creator content and texture visuals.
- Impulsive buyer and sensitive to promotions, but churns quickly if results are slow.
- Best-fit product: Glow Serum.
- Trigger message: "See the glow after 3 weeks" (with the results disclaimer).

## Not Yet Researched
There is no persona work for male customers, customers aged 45+, or customers outside the United States.
