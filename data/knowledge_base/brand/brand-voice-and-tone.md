# Lumera Brand Voice and Tone Guidelines

## Voice Pillars
Lumera speaks with three pillars: **Calm**, **Credible**, and **Kind**.
- Calm: no hype, no urgency theatre, no shouting. Short sentences and generous white space.
- Credible: every benefit is tied to an ingredient or to approved evidence. We explain, we do not exaggerate.
- Kind: we never shame skin. We do not talk about "flaws", "ugly pores", or "fixing" anyone.

## Writing Rules
- Reading level: aim for grade 8 or lower. Prefer plain words over jargon; explain an ingredient the first time it appears.
- At most one emoji per post or ad. No all-caps words. At most one exclamation mark per piece.
- Use "helps", "visibly", and "looks" when describing benefits (for example "helps skin look brighter").
- Preferred vocabulary: glow, barrier, hydrated, balanced, comfortable, gentle, routine.
- Words to avoid: miracle, flawless, perfect skin, detox, anti-aging, "instant results", "magic", "cure", "fix".

## Meta Ads Copy Conventions
- Headline: 40 characters or fewer.
- Primary text: the first line must deliver the hook within 125 characters, because the rest is truncated in feed.
- Preferred calls to action: "Shop now", "Try it for 30 days", "See how it works". Do not use "Buy now before it's gone" style urgency.
- Always write in first person singular for creator-style scripts ("I") and first person plural for brand copy ("we").

## Example On-Brand Lines
- "Meet a serum that plays nicely with sensitive skin."
- "Three steps. Zero drama."
- "Glow, without the stinging."

## Example Off-Brand Lines
- "Say goodbye to ugly dark spots FOREVER!!!"
- "The miracle serum dermatologists don't want you to know about."
