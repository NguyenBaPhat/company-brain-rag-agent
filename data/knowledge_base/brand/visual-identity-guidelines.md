# Lumera Visual Identity Guidelines

## Color Palette
- Warm Ivory `#F6F1E9` (primary background)
- Sage `#8FA68E` (accent, buttons)
- Deep Clay `#7A4B3A` (headlines and logo)
- Soft Charcoal `#2E2B28` (body text)
Do not use neon colors, gradients, or pure black backgrounds.

## Typography
- Headlines: Fraunces (serif), sentence case only.
- Body and UI: Inter.
- Minimum text size on mobile video: 36 px equivalent, with high contrast against the background.

## Photography and Video
- Natural window light. Show real skin texture; no heavy retouching or plastic-looking smoothing filters.
- Product shots: on warm ivory or soft stone surfaces, with a single plant or fabric prop at most.
- Video: always burn in captions (more than 80% of paid social views are muted). First 3 seconds must show either the product in use or a face, never a logo-only opening.
- Creators and models should reflect a range of ages, skin tones and skin types.

## Logo Usage
- Keep clear space equal to the height of the letter "L" on all sides.
- Minimum logo width: 80 px on digital.
- Never place the logo on a busy photo background without a solid overlay.
