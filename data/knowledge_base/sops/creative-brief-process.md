# SOP — Creative Brief Process

## When A Brief Is Required
Every new paid-social creative concept, influencer campaign, or landing-page refresh needs a one-page brief before production starts.

## Required Brief Template
1. **Objective** — one measurable goal (for example "lower CAC for Glow Serum cold audiences below $35").
2. **Audience** — a named persona from the research library and the funnel stage.
3. **Key message** — a single sentence. One brief equals one message.
4. **Proof points** — each proof point must cite the source document (product fact sheet, study, or research).
5. **Mandatory claims, disclaimers and prohibited claims** — copied from the product fact sheet and the compliance policy.
6. **Hook ideas** — three distinct hooks for the first 3 seconds.
7. **Format and placement** — for example 15-second vertical video for Reels and Stories, or a carousel.
8. **Call to action** — chosen from the approved CTA list in the brand voice guidelines.
9. **Success metrics and test plan** — primary KPI, minimum spend and run time, and a kill criterion.

## Approval Workflow
1. Brand Manager review — 1 business day.
2. Compliance review — 2 business days. A brief cannot move to production without compliance sign-off.
3. Performance Lead review — 1 business day.
The total expected cycle is 4 business days. Rush requests can skip no step; they can only be prioritised in the queue.

## Naming Convention
Assets and ad names follow `LUM_[PRODUCT]_[ANGLE]_[FORMAT]_[YYMM]`, for example `LUM_GLOW_PROBLEMFIRST_UGC15_2602`.

## Testing Rules
- Test one variable at a time (hook, format, or offer).
- Each variant needs a minimum of $1,500 spend or 7 days before a decision.
- Retire a creative when its frequency exceeds 3.5 or CTR drops 30% from its first week.
