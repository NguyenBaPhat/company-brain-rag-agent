# Shipping, Returns and Subscription Policy (2026)

## Shipping
- Free standard shipping on orders of $60 or more. Orders below $60 pay a flat $5.95.
- Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within the United States. Express (1–2 business days) costs $14.
- We currently ship to the United States and Canada only. Other regions are not supported yet.

## Returns and Satisfaction Guarantee
- 30-day satisfaction guarantee: a full refund on the first purchase of each product, including opened products.
- After 30 days, unopened products can be returned for store credit.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## Subscribe & Save
- 15% off every subscription order.
- Customers can skip, change frequency, or cancel at any time in the account portal, but changes must be made at least 48 hours before the renewal date.
- Subscription renewals are charged at the price at the time of renewal.

## Promotions
Discount codes cannot be stacked with Subscribe & Save. The Starter Trio bundle ($79) is not eligible for additional discount codes.
