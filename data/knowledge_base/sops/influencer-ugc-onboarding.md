# SOP — Influencer and UGC Creator Onboarding

## Creator Tiers
- Nano (1k–10k followers): gifted product only, affiliate code.
- Micro (10k–100k): gifted product plus affiliate commission.
- UGC-only creators (no posting on their own channel): flat fee per approved video, with content licensed to Lumera.

## Onboarding Steps
1. Send the creator brief and the approved claims list for the product.
2. Ship the seeding kit (full-size product plus a Starter Trio card). Target dispatch within 3 business days.
3. Creator signs the content agreement, which includes disclosure obligations.
4. Creator submits the draft for approval at least 72 hours before the planned posting time.
5. Brand Manager and Compliance approve the draft. Creators must not post before approval.

## Disclosure Requirements
All content must include the platform paid-partnership label and #ad or #gifted in the first line of the caption. See the claims and advertising policy.

## Commission and Usage Rights
- Affiliate commission: 12% of net sales via the creator's code.
- Paid-usage rights (running the creator's video as an ad on Lumera accounts): 90 days from first ad run, extendable by agreement.

## Do And Do Not
- Do encourage creators to share their genuine routine and timeframe ("after 3 weeks").
- Do not allow creators to make claims outside the approved list, to show personal medical conditions as "cured", or to compare Lumera against named brands.
