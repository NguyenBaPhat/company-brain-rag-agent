# SOP — Customer Support Escalation and Adverse Reactions

## Tone
Be warm and factual. Never diagnose a skin condition and never give medical advice. Suggest the customer stops using the product and contacts a doctor if the reaction is severe.

## Adverse Reaction Handling
1. Express empathy and ask the customer to stop using the product.
2. Collect the product name, the batch or lot code printed on the pack, a photo of the reaction if the customer is comfortable sharing, and the date of first use.
3. Log an Adverse Event ticket in the helpdesk within 24 hours and tag it `adverse-event`.
4. Escalate to the Quality team on the same business day. Do not wait for the next stand-up.
5. Offer a full refund immediately. The customer does not need to return the product.

## Refund Authorisation Limits
- Support agents may approve refunds up to $100.
- Refunds above $100 need Support Lead approval.
- Suspected fraud (more than 3 refunds in 90 days from one customer) must go to the Support Lead.

## Escalation Matrix
- Damaged or leaking parcel: replace or refund, no photo needed under $50.
- Public social media complaint: reply within 4 business hours and move the conversation to direct messages.
- Press or legal enquiries: forward to Legal and Compliance, do not respond directly.

## Support Response Targets
First reply within 12 hours on business days. Resolution within 2 business days for standard queries.
