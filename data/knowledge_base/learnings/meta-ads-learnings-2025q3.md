# Meta Ads Creative Learnings — Q3 2025

Scope: July–September 2025 on Meta (Facebook and Instagram). Total spend $412k across 38 creatives for Glow Serum and Barrier Cream.

## Format Performance
- UGC-style 15-second vertical video: average CTR 1.9%.
- Studio-produced video: average CTR 1.1%.
- Ingredient-explainer carousel: conversion rate 3.4% in retargeting audiences, but weak with cold audiences.

## Hook Performance
- Problem-first hooks ("My skin barrier was wrecked") averaged CTR 2.1%, the best of all hook types.
- Testimonials that state a specific timeframe ("after 3 weeks") produced a 24% higher thumb-stop rate than testimonials without one.
- Founder-story hooks underperformed with CTR 0.9%.

## Offer Performance
- Discount-led creative: CAC $29, but 90-day repurchase rate only 21%.
- Education-led creative: CAC $41, 90-day repurchase rate 34%.

## Creative Fatigue
Performance dropped sharply once ad frequency passed 3.5, typically around day 14 of a run. Plan at least two fresh variants per two weeks per audience.

## Compliance Outcomes
Three ads were rejected by Meta for implied health claims (two used "clears breakouts" wording and one used "dermatologist approved"). All three were rewritten with appearance-based language and re-approved.

## Gaps
No learnings exist yet for TikTok paid ads, Google Search, or non-US markets. Sample sizes for Barrier Cream creatives were small (6 creatives).
