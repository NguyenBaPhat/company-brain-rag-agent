# Hook and Format Learnings — Organic and Creator Content 2025

## What Worked
- **Texture and ASMR close-ups** of the Glow Serum pump and gel held 68% of viewers past 3 seconds on Reels and TikTok, and resonate most with the Glow-Seeking Gia persona.
- **"What I stopped using" routine-simplification videos** (creators swap five products for three) drove the highest saves and shares, and speak to the Routine-Minimalist Minh persona.
- **Myth-versus-fact statics** (for example "Does vitamin C sting?") delivered medium engagement and a high comment rate that doubles as social proof.

## What Did Not Work
- Videos longer than 45 seconds underperformed with cold audiences; completion rate under 12%.
- Founder-led talking-head videos had low engagement compared with creator-led videos.
- Hard-sell "limited time" urgency posts reduced engagement and conflicted with the brand voice.

## Creator Guidance
- Videos with burned-in captions had 14% higher average watch time than those without.
- Creators who mention a real timeframe and their own skin experience perform better than scripted claims.
- Always show the product in use within the first 3 seconds.

## Testing Cadence Recommendation
Run one hook test per week using the same body of the video, changing only the first 3 seconds, as per the one-variable rule in the creative brief SOP.
