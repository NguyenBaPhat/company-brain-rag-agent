# Lumera Barrier Cream — Product Fact Sheet

## Overview
Barrier Cream is a rich-but-not-greasy daily moisturizer launched in September 2024. Size: 50 ml jar. Retail price: $34. Subscribe & Save price: $28.90 (15% off).

## Key Ingredients
- Ceramide NP, Ceramide AP, and Ceramide EOP complex
- 2% Panthenol (pro-vitamin B5)
- Squalane (plant-derived)
- Glycerin
- Fragrance-free, vegan, cruelty-free.

## How To Use
Apply a pearl-sized amount to face and neck morning and/or night, after serum. Suitable for daily use. Not intended for use on broken skin.

## Approved Benefit Claims
- "Helps support the skin's moisture barrier."
- "Ceramide-enriched formula for lasting hydration."
- "Comfortable for dry and sensitive-feeling skin."

## Evidence Status
No consumer or clinical study has been completed for Barrier Cream. Therefore no percentage-based result claims, "clinically proven", or "visible results in X weeks" claims may be made for this product. Customer review sentiment (see review themes research) may be used only as qualitative social proof with permission.

## Claims That Are NOT Allowed
- "Repairs", "heals", or "treats" eczema, dermatitis, rosacea, or damaged skin.
- "Clinically proven" or "dermatologist recommended".
- Claims that it can replace prescription treatments.
