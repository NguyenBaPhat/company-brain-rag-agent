# Lumera Glow Serum — Product Fact Sheet

## Overview
Glow Serum is Lumera Skin's hero product, launched in March 2024. It is a lightweight, non-sticky gel serum designed for people with dull-looking or uneven-looking skin who also have sensitivity concerns. Size: 30 ml. Retail price: $38. Subscribe & Save price: $32.30 (15% off).

## Key Ingredients
- 10% 3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid (a stable vitamin C derivative, gentler than L-ascorbic acid)
- 4% Niacinamide
- 0.5% Sodium Hyaluronate (multi-weight hyaluronic acid)
- Formula pH: 5.5. Fragrance-free, vegan, cruelty-free, no essential oils.

## How To Use
Apply 2 pumps to clean skin every morning, before moisturizer and sunscreen. Evening use is optional. Patch test on the inner forearm for 24 hours before first use. Always follow with SPF 30+ during the day. Shelf life: 12 months after opening (12M PAO symbol on the pack).

## Approved Benefit Claims
- "Helps visibly brighten the look of dull skin."
- "Helps skin look more even-toned."
- "Hydrating, lightweight gel texture that layers well under sunscreen and makeup."
- "Formulated without fragrance and essential oils for sensitive-skin routines."

## Substantiated Results (Consumer Perception Study)
A 4-week independent consumer perception study (n=64, ages 22–45, self-assessment) found:
- 82% said their skin looked brighter.
- 76% said their skin felt more hydrated.
- 69% said their skin tone looked more even.
Any use of these figures must carry the disclaimer "Based on a 4-week consumer perception study (n=64). Individual results may vary."

## Claims That Are NOT Allowed
- Any claim that the serum treats, cures, or heals hyperpigmentation, melasma, acne, or any skin condition.
- "Clinically proven" or "dermatologist tested/recommended" (no clinical trial or dermatologist endorsement exists).
- "Permanently fades dark spots" or any permanence claim.
- Safety claims for pregnancy or breastfeeding. Direct customers to consult their doctor.
