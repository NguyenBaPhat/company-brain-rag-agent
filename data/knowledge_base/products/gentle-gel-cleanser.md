# Lumera Gentle Gel Cleanser — Product Fact Sheet

## Overview
Gentle Gel Cleanser is a low-foam, pH-balanced daily cleanser. Size: 150 ml. Retail price: $22. Launched in June 2025 as the entry point of the range.

## Key Ingredients
- Mild amino-acid based surfactants (sulfate-free)
- Glycerin
- Allantoin
- pH 5.5, fragrance-free, vegan, cruelty-free.

## How To Use
Massage onto damp skin for 30 seconds, rinse with lukewarm water. Use morning and evening. Avoid the eye area. It removes light makeup and sunscreen but is not designed to remove waterproof mascara.

## Approved Benefit Claims
- "Cleanses without that tight, stripped feeling."
- "Gentle, sulfate-free formula for sensitive-feeling skin."

## Bundle
The Starter Trio bundles Glow Serum ($38), Barrier Cream ($34) and Gentle Gel Cleanser ($22). Bundle price: $79 (individual total $94, a saving of $15).

## Claims That Are NOT Allowed
- "Clears acne", "treats breakouts", or any acne-related treatment claim.
- "Dermatologist recommended" or "clinically proven".
