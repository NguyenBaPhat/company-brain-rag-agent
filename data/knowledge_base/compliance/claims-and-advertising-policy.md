# Claims and Advertising Compliance Policy

This policy applies to every ad, social post, email, landing page, influencer script, and creative brief produced for Lumera Skin. Lumera products are cosmetics, not drugs. We may describe how skin looks and feels; we may not claim to treat, cure, prevent, or heal any disease or medical condition.

## 1. Prohibited Disease and Treatment Claims
Never say or imply that a product treats, cures, heals, repairs, or prevents acne, eczema, dermatitis, rosacea, psoriasis, melasma, hyperpigmentation disorders, or any other skin condition. Use appearance language instead: "helps skin look more even", "helps support the skin's moisture barrier".

## 2. Endorsement and Proof Claims
- "Clinically proven", "clinically tested", "dermatologist recommended", "dermatologist approved", and "doctor approved" are prohibited. Lumera has no clinical trial and no dermatologist endorsement. (The "dermatologist recommended" line was retired in March 2025 after a compliance audit.)
- Percentage-based results may be used only for Glow Serum, and only from the 4-week consumer perception study (n=64). They must include the disclaimer "Individual results may vary."
- No result statistics may be used for Barrier Cream or Gentle Gel Cleanser because no study exists.

## 3. Superlatives and Comparative Claims
- Words such as "best", "#1", "miracle", "perfect", "flawless", or "guaranteed results" require substantiation we do not have and are prohibited.
- Do not name competitors in any creative. Do not use comparative claims ("better than", "stronger than") without prior Legal approval.

## 4. Before-and-After Content
- Before-and-after images must be unretouched, shot in the same lighting, angle, and with no filters.
- The timeframe between photos must be stated and must be at least 4 weeks.
- They must carry the disclaimer "Individual results may vary."
- Before-and-after images are not allowed in cold-audience Meta ads. They are only allowed on owned channels and in retargeting.

## 5. Influencer and UGC Disclosure
- All paid, gifted, or affiliate content must be clearly disclosed: use the platform's paid partnership label and include #ad or #gifted in the first line of the caption.
- Creators may only use claims from the approved claims lists in the product fact sheets.
- Creators must not describe personal medical conditions as having been resolved by the product (for example "it cured my eczema").

## 6. Sensitive Audiences and Body Image
- Do not target or call out personal attributes such as skin condition, age, or insecurities in ad copy (for example "Struggling with ugly pores?"). Meta policy and our brand voice both prohibit this.
- Do not make pregnancy or breastfeeding safety claims. Say "consult your doctor".

## 7. Review SLA and Escalation
Compliance reviews every new creative brief and every final asset. Standard turnaround is 2 business days. If a claim is not covered by this policy or by a product fact sheet, treat it as not approved and escalate to Legal and Compliance (compliance@lumeraskin.example) before use.
