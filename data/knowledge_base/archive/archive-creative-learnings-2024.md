# Creative Learnings 2024 (ARCHIVED)

This document is archived and kept only for history. Several statements are no longer valid.

## 2024 Findings
- "Dermatologist recommended" in the headline lifted CTR by 18% in test ads. (This claim was retired in March 2025 and is no longer permitted.)
- Glow Serum retail price was $32 in 2024. (The current price is $38.)
- Free shipping threshold was $50. (See the current shipping policy.)
- Best-performing format in 2024 was studio-produced product video with a voice-over, CTR 1.4%.

## Why It Is Archived
Claims, prices and format learnings changed during 2025. Use the 2025 learnings and the current policies instead.
